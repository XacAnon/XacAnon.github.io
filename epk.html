Biography

Short bio: Xac Anon is a lo-fi emo rapper from Newcastle, Australia, making dark, honest music about grief, heartbreak and survival.

Xac Anon is a lo-fi emo rapper from Newcastle, NSW, whose music blends raw emotion with haunting production. His songs sit in the quiet hours of the night: slow, dark and honest, turning grief, guilt and heartbreak into something listeners can hold onto.

His work, including the album Purgatory (2025) and the Darkness & Light EP, carries the echo of pain and transcendence. Since 2023 he has released a steady run of singles, from June Storms to Olympus, Hell, Pt. 3 and Hotel, working with artists including Dayze, Incognetoed, GCade and Jack Anon.
